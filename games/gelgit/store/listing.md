# Gelgit (Tide) — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Tide: Sand Castle Defense
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Build walls, outsmart the waves
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Build sand walls, see where the wave will reach, and save your castles.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** sand,castle,beach,wave,sea,tide,wall,defense,puzzle,strategy,turn,relax,offline
- **Description:**

  The sea is coming for your sand castles. Hold it back!

  ▸ Tap the sand to raise a wall, tap again to take it back
  ▸ A live preview shows exactly how far this round's wave will reach
  ▸ Press Wave! and watch the water flow around your walls
  ▸ Every round the sea reaches one step further and walls wear down

  How long can you keep your castles dry?
  • Turn-based and fair: nothing random happens after you press Wave
  • Endless rounds, best score, one rebuild per game
  • Daily missions and a 7-day reward calendar
  • Six beach themes to unlock: tropical, sunset, coral, arctic, moonlight
  • Calm beach visuals, soft surf sounds and haptics
  • No timer, plays offline
