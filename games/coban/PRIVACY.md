# Çoban (Herd) — Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

_Last updated / Son güncelleme: 2026-10-10_

on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob; AdMob may collect device identifiers
(such as the advertising ID) and approximate location to show and measure ads, subject to your
consent (EU/UK consent form, iOS App Tracking Transparency). See Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or
Google Play; we never see your payment details. Anonymous gameplay events (e.g. "level 12 completed")
may be recorded to improve the game. Contact: mrtbnymntskn@gmail.com
