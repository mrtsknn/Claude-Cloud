# Çoban (Herd) — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Herd: Sheepdog Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Bark, herd & pen the flock
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Steer your sheepdog, bark to scatter the flock and pen every animal in time.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** sheep,herd,sheepdog,dog,bark,farm,flock,shepherd,pen,goat,goose,casual,endless,offline
- **Description:**

  Be the shepherd. Drag your finger and your sheepdog runs to it — the flock runs from the dog.
  Steer every animal through the gate before the clock runs out.

  ▸ One-finger control, learn it in three seconds
  ▸ BARK! Send a shockwave that scatters the flock toward the pen
  ▸ New friends and troubles: lambs that follow their mother, goats that hop fences,
    speedy geese, sticky mud, and a pen gate your dog must unlock
  ▸ 120 meadows, every one tested to be solvable in time
  ▸ Endless Rush: the flock keeps coming — how many can you pen before three get away?
  ▸ Train your dog: speed, bark range and bark recharge
  ▸ 7 sheepdogs to unlock: Border Collie, Golden, Kangal, Husky, Shiba and more
  ▸ Daily missions, a 7-day reward calendar, share your best runs
  ▸ Plays offline
