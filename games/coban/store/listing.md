# Çoban (Herd) — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Herd: Sheepdog Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Guide the flock to the pen
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Drag to steer your sheepdog and pen every sheep before time runs out.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** sheep,herd,sheepdog,dog,farm,flock,shepherd,pen,casual,puzzle,animal,relax,offline
- **Description:**

  Be the shepherd. Drag your finger and your sheepdog runs to it — the sheep run from the dog.
  Steer the whole flock through the gate before the clock runs out.

  ▸ One-finger control, learn it in three seconds
  ▸ 120 generated meadows, each checked to be solvable: ponds, fences, bushes
  ▸ A stubborn black sheep and sneaky wolves on later levels — chase them off!
  ▸ Earn up to 3 stars by finishing fast
  ▸ 7 sheepdogs to unlock: Border Collie, Golden, Kangal, Husky, Shiba and more
  ▸ Daily missions and a 7-day reward calendar
  ▸ Plays offline
