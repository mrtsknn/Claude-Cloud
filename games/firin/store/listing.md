# Fırın / Bake Rush — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Bake Rush: Bakery Time Manager
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Bake golden, serve fast
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Bake bread, ring bread and cakes, serve customers before they leave!
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** bakery,cooking,bake,bread,cake,serve,customer,time,rush,restaurant,chef,arcade,casual
- **Description:**

  Your bakery is open and the line is growing! Tap the dough, watch it turn golden in the oven,
  and hand it over before the customer runs out of patience — but don't let it burn.

  ▸ One-tap controls: dough → oven → customer
  ▸ 120 levels: more treats, more customers, hotter ovens
  ▸ Fast service earns tips, combos earn more
  ▸ Daily missions, a 7-day reward calendar and 6 bakery themes
  ▸ No energy system, play as long as you like
