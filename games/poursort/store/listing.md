## English

- **Name (30):** Pour Sort: Color Water Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Calm sorting, every level solvable
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Pour colors until each tube holds one. 500 fair levels, plays offline.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** water,sort,color,liquid,tube,bottle,puzzle,relax,brain,logic,ball,offline,calm
- **Description:**

  Tap a tube, tap another, and watch the color pour. Sort every color into its own tube.

  ▸ Simple: you can only pour onto the same color or into an empty tube
  ▸ 500 levels on a star map that grow from 2 to 12 colors — and it keeps going after that
  ▸ Every level is checked by a solver, so every one can be solved
  ▸ Earn up to 3 stars: finish without help for a perfect score
  ▸ Daily missions, a 7-day reward calendar and coins to spend
  ▸ 6 tube styles: Classic, Pastel, Lagoon, Jewel, Retro and Neon
  ▸ No timer, no lives, plays offline

  Built to be fair
  • Free undos, a free hint and a free extra tube in every level
  • Hints come from a real solver, not a guess
  • Color symbols mode for color-blind players
  • Smooth pouring animations, gentle sounds and haptics
