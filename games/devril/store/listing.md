# Devril / Topple — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Topple: Domino Chain Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Turn the tiles, push once
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Turn each domino so one push topples them all. Calm, clever, fully offline.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** domino,chain,topple,puzzle,logic,brain,arrow,rotate,tiles,relax,offline,stars
- **Description:**

  Every tile points somewhere. Tap to turn them, then push the first one and watch the chain run.
  Topple every tile with a single push.

  ▸ One rule: a falling tile knocks over the tile it points at
  ▸ 200 levels from a tiny 4×4 to a winding 8×8, with pinned tiles that won't turn
  ▸ Beat par for three stars, every level has a proven solution
  ▸ No timer, no lives, fully offline
  ▸ Daily missions, a 7-day reward calendar and six tile themes — oak, walnut, marble, jade, candy, midnight

  Made to feel good
  • Wooden tiles that click as the chain climbs a little melody
  • Instant reset after a miss, undo and a free hint in every level
  • Gentle haptics, smooth animations, works one-handed
