# Çark (Cogwork) — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Cogwork: Gear Chain Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Connect gears, start the clocks
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Drag gears to link the motor to every clock — the right way round. Calm and offline.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** gear,cog,puzzle,logic,brain,clock,machine,connect,chain,relax,offline,steampunk,mechanic
- **Description:**

  Drag gears onto the pegs and link the motor to every clock. Meshed gears turn in opposite
  directions, so each clock must be reached the right way round: ↻ or ↺.

  ▸ One rule you already know: the next gear turns the other way
  ▸ Belts flip the spin — but close a loop the wrong way and everything jams
  ▸ 150 levels, from one clock to three, every one checked by a solver
  ▸ Earn stars by using the fewest gears

  Made to be fair
  • Unlimited undo, tap a gear to take it back
  • A free hint in every level
  • No timer, no lives, fully offline
  • Smooth spinning gears, ticking sounds and haptics
