# Makas / Switchyard — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Switchyard: Train Switch Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Flip switches, route trains
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Tap the switches and send every colored train to its matching station.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** train,rail,switch,track,railway,puzzle,traffic,station,color,route,reflex,brain
- **Description:**

  Colored trains roll out of the tunnel. Tap a switch to flip it and guide each train to the
  station of its color. Wrong station or a crash costs a heart — keep three and earn three stars.

  ▸ One-tap controls, learn it in seconds
  ▸ 150 levels: more colors, more switches, faster trains, two tunnels
  ▸ Every level is verified to be solvable
  ▸ Color + shape symbols on every train and station
  ▸ Daily missions, a 7-day reward calendar and 7 train themes
  ▸ Plays offline
