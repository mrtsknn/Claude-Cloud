# Çömlek — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Clay: Relaxing Pottery
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Shape, paint & collect pots
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Throw wet clay, paint patterns, fill your shelf. Calm, satisfying pottery.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** pottery,clay,wheel,vase,relax,asmr,ceramic,shape,craft,calm,satisfying,art
- **Description:**

  Put your fingers on the spinning wet clay and feel it move. Squeeze it tall, pull it out wide,
  fire it in the kiln, then paint rings and patterns while it spins. Every pot lands on your
  collection shelf — share your favourites.

  ▸ Deeply satisfying: glossy wet clay, throwing rings, a soft wheel hum and haptic ticks
  ▸ Press in to raise it, drag out from inside to open wide, low bowls
  ▸ Customer orders: 120 requests — the closer the match, the bigger the reward
  ▸ Decorate on the wheel: 8 paints, dots, waves, zigzags and leaves
  ▸ Free studio: no target, just make anything and keep it
  ▸ Collection shelf and one-tap sharing of your pots as pictures
  ▸ 8 glazes to collect, daily missions and rewards, no timer, plays offline
