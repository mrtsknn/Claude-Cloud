# Çömlek — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Clay: Relaxing Pottery
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Shape, fire and glaze pots
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Press the spinning clay into vases, bowls and pitchers. Calm and satisfying.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** pottery,clay,wheel,vase,relax,asmr,ceramic,shape,craft,calm,satisfying,art
- **Description:**

  Put your fingers on the spinning clay and shape it. Match the dashed silhouette, fire it in
  the kiln and pick a glaze for your new pot.

  ▸ One touch control: press in from either side, clay rises as you squeeze
  ▸ 120 pots: cups, bowls, vases and pitchers with necks, bellies and lips
  ▸ Live match score — 70%, 82% and 92% earn one, two or three stars
  ▸ 8 glazes to collect: celadon, cobalt stripes, Iznik dots, obsidian & gold…
  ▸ Daily missions and daily rewards, no timer, plays offline
