# Kristal — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Snowflake: Symmetry Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** One tap, six reflections
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Tap crystals to match the snowflake. Calm symmetry puzzles, play offline.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** snowflake,symmetry,crystal,lights out,logic,puzzle,relax,calm,brain,hexagon,offline,zen
- **Description:**

  Every tap appears six times. Shape a glowing snowflake until it matches the target.

  ▸ Simple: tap a crystal to switch it on or off, in every reflection at once
  ▸ 150 hand-tuned levels: neighbour ripples from level 15, mirror symmetry from level 40
  ▸ Every level is solvable, with a proven minimum number of taps (par)
  ▸ No timer, no lives, plays offline

  Calm by design
  • Earn up to three stars by matching par, track them on the level map
  • Unlimited undo, a free hint in every level, a target outline when you need it
  • Daily missions and a 7-day reward calendar
  • Six crystal themes to collect: ice, aurora, rose quartz, amber, emerald, midnight
  • Glassy sounds, gentle haptics, soft snowfall
  • The finished snowflake spins and drifts away
