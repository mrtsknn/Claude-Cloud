## English

- **Name (30):** Balance: Calm Weight Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Hang weights, level the plank
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Hang every weight so the plank sits perfectly level. Calm, fair, offline.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** balance,scale,weight,torque,lever,physics,logic,math,puzzle,brain,calm,relax,offline
- **Description:**

  A plank rests on a pivot. Drag weights onto its pegs — the farther out, the stronger the pull.
  Use every weight and bring the plank perfectly level.

  ▸ One rule: weight × distance must match on both sides
  ▸ 150 levels on a gentle ramp, from two weights to stacked pegs and blocked slots
  ▸ Every level is built from a real solution, so every one can be solved
  ▸ No timer, no lives, fully offline

  Calm by design
  • A bubble level and side totals show exactly where you stand
  • Tap any weight to take it back, undo anytime
  • A free hint in every level
  • Soft stone-and-brass look, wooden knocks and gentle chimes

  More to come back for
  • Level map with stars, three daily missions and a 7-day reward calendar
  • Six materials for your scale: brass, wood, marble, candy, stone and jade
  • Optional videos for extra coins; one-time purchase removes ads
