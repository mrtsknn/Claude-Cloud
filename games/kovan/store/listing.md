# Kovan — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Hive Merge: Idle Bee Tycoon
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Merge bees, make sweet honey
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Drag bees together, grow your honeycomb hive and earn honey even while away.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** merge,idle,bee,honey,hive,tycoon,clicker,incremental,relax,offline,evolve,cute,collect
- **Description:**

  Hatch an egg, drag a bee onto its twin and watch them merge into a bigger, busier bee.
  Every bee makes honey every second — even while you are away.

  ▸ 30 bees to discover, from Larva to the Hive Empress
  ▸ 4 worlds — Meadow, Orchard, Forest, Mountain — each with its own hive, all making honey at once
  ▸ A 40-step quest chain with a reward on every step
  ▸ Weekly flower festivals: a boosted bee and a limited festival bee to collect
  ▸ Honeycomb hives that grow from 20 to 42 cells
  ▸ Better eggs, bigger hive, more honey
  ▸ Gift box for every new bee you discover
  ▸ Up to 4 hours of offline honey (double it with a video)
  ▸ Daily missions, 7-day rewards and 7 hive themes

  Calm, satisfying, playable with one thumb. Optional reminders when your hive is full.
