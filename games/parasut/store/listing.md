# Paraşüt (Skydrop) — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Skydrop: Parachute Landing
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** One tap. Hit the boat.
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Open your chute, ride the wind and land on moving boats. One-tap arcade fun.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** parachute,skydive,landing,one tap,arcade,wind,boat,jump,endless,casual,timing,bullseye
- **Description:**

  Jump from the plane, tap to open your parachute and drift with the wind onto a moving boat.
  Tap again to cut away and drop fast — once per jump. Miss the deck and it's a splash!

  ▸ One-tap controls, learned in three seconds
  ▸ Bullseye landings score double; chain them for combos
  ▸ Endless mode: wind gets stronger, decks smaller, boats faster
  ▸ 100 challenge levels with stars
  ▸ Every jump is checked by a simulation — always landable
  ▸ Daily rewards, missions and 8 parachute styles
  ▸ Plays offline
