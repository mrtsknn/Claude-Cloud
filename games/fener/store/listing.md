# Fener — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Lamplight: Night Town Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Light every window exactly once
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Place street lamps so every window glows exactly once. Calm, clever, offline.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** logic,puzzle,lamp,light,window,brain,relax,calm,akari,night,town,offline,place
- **Description:**

  Night falls over a small town. Drag lamps onto the streets and light every window — exactly once.

  ▸ Each lamp shines in its own pattern: plus, line, column or diagonal
  ▸ A window lit twice glows red, so you always see your mistake
  ▸ 200 levels, from 6×6 to 9×9, every one solvable
  ▸ No timer, no lives, plays offline

  More to light up
  • Level map with stars, three daily missions and a 7-day reward calendar
  • Six lamp-light and night-sky themes: Amber, Moonlight, Firefly, Lavender, Harbor, Ember
  • Earn coins by solving towns; optional videos double your reward

  Made to be calm
  • Warm glow, soft chimes and gentle haptics
  • Undo anytime; the first hint in every level is free, more for coins or a short video
  • Earn up to three stars for clean solves
