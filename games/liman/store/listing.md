# Liman — Mağaza Metinleri

## English

- **Name (30):** Harbor Load: Cargo Puzzle
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Stack smart, keep the ship level
- **Short description (Play, 80):** Load containers port by port and keep the ship balanced. Calm, fair, offline.
- **Keywords (iOS, 100):** cargo,ship,container,harbor,port,stack,load,puzzle,logic,brain,balance,sort,offline
- **Description:**

  Tap a container on the dock, tap a spot on the ship, and the crane drops it in.
  Then press Sail and watch the ship unload at every port.

  ▸ Two simple rules: the next port's boxes must be on top, and the ship must stay level
  ▸ Mistakes glow red and the ship leans as you load, so you learn by playing
  ▸ 150 levels from 2 ports and 4 boxes to 4 ports and 14 boxes
  ▸ Every level is checked by a solver, so every one can be solved
  ▸ No timer, no lives, fully offline

  Fair by design
  • A free hint in every level, hints come from a real solver
  • Unlimited undo
  • Every port has its own color and shape, friendly to color-blind players
  • Smooth crane animations, gentle sounds and haptics
